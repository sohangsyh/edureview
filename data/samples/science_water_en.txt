Water and Solutions

Water can exist in three states: solid ice, liquid water and water vapor. When ice is heated it melts, and when water is heated it evaporates.

Matter is made of tiny particles. Particles get bigger when they are heated.

Salt dissolves in water to form a solution. In this example, salt is the solute and water is the solvent.

Experiment: Pour 100 milliliters of salt solution into an evaporating dish. Notice the color of the solution. Heat the dish with a Bunsen burner until all the water has evaporated, leaving salt crystals.
